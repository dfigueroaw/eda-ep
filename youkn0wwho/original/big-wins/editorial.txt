Goal: maximise  median(subarray) − minimum(subarray)
. Treat the minimum as the "left pointer" mn
 that we increase from 1
 up to max ai
. For every fixed mn
 we try to push a second pointer med
 (the candidate median) as high as possible while some subarray exists whose minimum is exactly mn
 and whose median is at least med
. The answer is the largest difference med − mn
 reached during this sweep.

Fix a value mn
. Any subarray whose minimum equals mn
 must contain at least one position u
 with au = mn
, and the whole subarray must lie inside the maximal span where that u
 is the strict minimum. Those spans are computed once by the usual monotone stack: lu=
 first index to the left that has value< a[u]+ 1
, ru=
first index to the right that has value< au− 1
.

For a potential median M
 define an array signi
 = +1
 if ai ≥ M
, −1
 otherwise. Inside any interval its median is ≥ M
 exactly when the total sum of sign over that interval is positive (at least half the elements are high). Therefore, for a particular position u
 to serve as the minimum we need

maxSuffix(lu, u−1)+signu+maxPrefix(u+1, ru)≥ 0
.

Here maxSuffix
 and maxPrefix
 are, respectively, the largest suffix sum ending at u−1
 and the largest prefix sum starting at u+1
, measured in the sign array.

All max-prefix / max-suffix / total-sum queries can be answered in O(log n)
 with a segment-tree that stores at each node (ans,bestPrefix,bestSuffix,totalSum
). Merging two children follows the standard Kadane-style formulas.

Algorithm loop. Initialise the segment tree with sign ≡ +1
, so total sum everywhere is positive; set med = 1
. For med←1
 we must exclude from the tree every position whose value is < med
, by updating that point to −1
. Now iterate mn
 from 1
 upward:

- for every index u
 with au = mn
 evaluate the inequality above.

- while the inequality is satisfied for all such indices, increment med
 by 1
 and for every position whose value just became < med
 perform one point-update to −1
 in the segment tree.

- when the test fails for some u
, stop raising med.

- record ans=max(ans,med − mn)
.

Because med only increases and never exceeds n
, the total number of tree updates is O(n)
 and every update / query costs O(log n)
, so the whole run is O(n log n)
. Memory is O(n)
.

Why correct. For each mn the inner while moves med upward greedily until the moment no subarray with minimum mn can keep the median≥ med
; thus the last valid value of med
 realises the true maximum for this mn
. The outer sweep examines every possible minimum, hence the largest gap ever recorded is the global optimum.
